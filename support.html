Help & support

Getting started

Swipe vertically in Watch to change videos. Tap the picture to pause or resume. Hold the picture to pause temporarily and hide the controls; release to restore them. Hold and drag the bottom timeline to seek. The sidebar controls speed, captions, video information and saved vocabulary.

Reading captions
The current Chinese sentence is paired with its English translation. The next sentence is dimmed. Tap a Chinese word to open its definition, pronunciation and save control. Use Dictionary to search Chinese, Pinyin or English, and Vocab to revisit saved words.

Your library
Explore shows thumbnails for the complete bundled catalog. The feed reshuffles and repeats; it is not an unlimited supply of unique videos. The current library works offline. More content can be added in app updates.

Reminders and privacy
Settings includes an optional saved-word reminder with a time picker. Today’s reminder is skipped once your daily goal is complete. The next four weeks are scheduled locally and refreshed when you use the app. Privacy & data lets you export your data or erase it and start over. If notifications were denied, enable them in iPhone Settings.

Report a caption or playback problem
Open the video's information sheet and note its title, creator and approximate time. Include the incorrect line and your suggested correction if you have one. Then use Contact support. No report is sent until you send the email yourself.

Contact

Daily plan and review
Settings → Daily plan shows your chosen routine. Vocab → Review flashcards lets you recall saved words before revealing their meaning. Remembered words return after longer intervals; difficult words return sooner. Listen & repeat plays on-device pronunciation for self-practice; it does not provide AI conversation or tone grading.
