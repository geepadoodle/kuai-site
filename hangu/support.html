Hangu support

Help with Korean videos and vocabulary.

Watching videos

Swipe vertically in Watch to change clips. Tap the video to pause or resume. Use the timeline to seek and the speed control to slow playback. Explore lets you browse clips and topics. Some content requires an internet connection and availability can change.

Captions and saved words

Follow Korean captions with romanization and English. Tap a subtitle word for its definition and save control. Open Words to search vocabulary and revisit saved words. Settings includes caption and feed preferences.

Reminders and learning data

Choose a reminder time in Settings. If notification permission was denied, change it in iPhone Settings. Privacy & data lets you export or erase local learning data. Exported files and device backups are not removed by an in-app reset.

Subscriptions

Use Membership to review plans or restore an existing purchase. To manage renewal, open Apple subscriptions. Erasing app data or deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Report a problem

Email turtle91021.gnps@gmail.com. For a caption or playback issue, include the clip title, creator, approximate time, and what happened. Your app version and iOS version can help. Do not send passwords or payment details.

For an account-data or privacy request, use the same support address and name the app you use.
