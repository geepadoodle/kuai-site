Accessibility in Kuai

Kuai supports system text sizing, VoiceOver labels and Reduce Motion. Captions have their own region beneath the video and can scroll vertically when more room is needed. English and Pinyin can be shown or hidden separately, and speech can play at a slower speed.

Use iPhone Settings → Accessibility for VoiceOver, larger text and Reduce Motion. In Kuai, use Settings for reading preferences and playback speed. The video and timeline expose playback and seeking actions to accessibility tools.

We welcome reports about barriers. Email turtle91021.gnps@gmail.com with the screen name, what happened and the accessibility setting you use. You do not need to share your learning history.
