Privacy policy

Kuai privacy policy
Updated November 5, 2026

Your learning stays on your device
Kuai stores your learning reasons, proficiency choice, achievement goal, content interests, learning challenges, checkpoint date, optional discovery source, daily goal, reading and playback preferences, saved vocabulary and review schedules, study-time and commitment choices, watched-video history and daily study time on your device. Kuai has no account and does not operate its own advertising, analytics SDK or cross-app tracking. Some clips use an embedded YouTube player; its third-party data handling is described below. We do not automatically upload your learning history or send it to an AI provider. Captions are prepared before publication; dictionary lookup and pronunciation use on-device resources.

Network access and YouTube playback
Most clips, thumbnails and the dictionary are bundled for offline use. Some clips stream through YouTube's embedded player and require an internet connection. To prepare and play those clips, including preloading upcoming clips, the app connects to YouTube and Google services. Those services receive connection and playback information, such as your IP address, device/browser information and requests identifying the video. The app uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain, but this does not make playback anonymous or prevent all third-party data collection. The player can store website data on your device and display YouTube branding or other provider content. Kuai does not send your saved vocabulary or local study history to YouTube. Google handles information under its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . YouTube terms: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms .

External links
Opening a creator's page, website or support email uses the selected external service and its own privacy practices. Local-data deletion clears Kuai's embedded-player website data; it does not delete records already held by Google, Apple or an email provider. Contact those providers for their retention and deletion options.

Notifications
Reminders are optional local notifications. Saved words, pronunciation and meanings may appear on your lock screen. You choose whether to allow them and can change their time or turn them off in Settings. At most one study reminder is scheduled each day, and today is skipped once your goal is complete. Up to four weeks are scheduled locally and refreshed when you use the app. An optional trial-ending reminder is separate. Kuai does not register a remote push token. Declining notification access does not limit learning.

Export and deletion
Settings → Privacy & data lets you export a readable JSON copy of your learning data or delete local data and restart onboarding. Deletion also clears app caches and scheduled reminders. It cannot remove exports you saved elsewhere or copies in device backups. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings. Kuai retains local learning records until you delete them or remove the app, subject to those backup settings.

Support
If you choose to email support, the recipient receives your email address and whatever you include. No learning history is attached automatically. Please do not include information you do not want to share. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the same address.

Subscriptions
Apple handles membership payment and provides product, transaction and entitlement information to the app. The app does not receive your payment-card details. An optional trial-ending reminder is scheduled locally after a verified eligible trial purchase.

New onboarding does not ask for age, regional background or heritage. Previously saved answers remain on the device until you erase them in Privacy & data.
