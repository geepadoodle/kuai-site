Privacy policy

Kuaide privacy policy
Updated November 13, 2026

Your learning stays on your device
Kuaide stores your learning reasons, proficiency choice, achievement goal, content interests, learning challenges, checkpoint date, optional discovery source, daily goal, reading and playback preferences, saved vocabulary and review schedules, study-time and commitment choices, watched-video history and daily study time on your device. Videos use an embedded YouTube player; its third-party data handling is described below. Captions are prepared before publication; dictionary lookup and pronunciation use on-device resources.

Optional account
You can use Kuaide without an account. If you choose Continue with Google or Sign in with Apple when available, the provider sends an account identifier, email address, and any profile name included in its sign-in response to our authentication provider, Supabase, so we can create and maintain your account. Google or Apple also processes your sign-in under its own privacy policy. If you choose Continue with email, we send your email address to Supabase to deliver a secure sign-in link and create or access your account. Kuaide stores your login session in this device’s Keychain. Signing in does not sync your saved words, watch history or study progress. Selected onboarding choices are sent automatically for aggregate product analytics. You can sign out or delete your account in Settings → Account. Account deletion removes the authentication account and related server records, then erases learning data on the device where you requested it. Apple subscription renewal must be canceled separately.


Onboarding analytics
When you complete onboarding, the app automatically sends your selected learning reasons, level, goals, challenges, topics, caption choice, study time and optional discovery source to our Supabase project once for aggregate counts. The report includes a random event ID to avoid duplicate submissions. It does not include your name, email, account ID, device ID, age, location, free-text answers, saved words or watch history. If delivery fails, the report remains on this device for retry; deleting local data removes that pending report. The server receives connection information including your IP address. We use a rotating hash of that address only to limit abuse; the stored answers are not linked to the hash or an account. Individual submitted reports cannot be found or deleted by account because no account identifier is attached. These reports help us understand learning preferences and referrals.


Network access and YouTube playback
Videos stream through YouTube's embedded player and require an internet connection. The dictionary, starter captions and thumbnails are bundled. Catalog updates are fetched from our public GitHub Pages site; its hosting provider receives connection information such as your IP address. Additional video thumbnails are requested from YouTube image servers (i.ytimg.com), which also receive connection information and the requested video identifier. No learning history is sent with catalog requests. To prepare and play those clips, including preloading upcoming clips, the app connects to YouTube and Google services. Those services receive connection and playback information, such as your IP address, device/browser information and requests identifying the video. The app uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain, but this does not make playback anonymous or prevent all third-party data collection. The player can store website data on your device and display YouTube branding, non-personalized advertising or other provider content. Google describes using embedded-service information for service delivery, security, diagnostics, analytics and advertising measurement; IP addresses can also indicate approximate location. Privacy-enhanced playback does not by itself establish that every form of advertising measurement is disabled. Kuaide does not send your saved vocabulary or local study history to YouTube. Google handles information under its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . YouTube terms: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms .

External links
Opening a creator's page, website or support email uses the selected external service and its own privacy practices. Local-data deletion clears Kuaide's embedded-player website data; it does not delete records already held by Google, Apple or an email provider. Contact those providers for their retention and deletion options.

Notifications
Reminders are optional local notifications. Saved words, pronunciation and meanings may appear on your lock screen. You choose whether to allow them and can change their time or turn them off in Settings. At most one study reminder is scheduled each day, and today is skipped once your goal is complete. Up to four weeks are scheduled locally and refreshed when you use the app. An optional trial-ending reminder is separate. Declining notification access does not limit learning.

Export and deletion
Settings → Privacy & data lets you export a readable JSON copy of your learning data or delete local data and restart onboarding. Deletion also clears app caches and scheduled reminders. It cannot remove exports you saved elsewhere or copies in device backups. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings. Kuaide retains local learning records until you delete them or remove the app, subject to those backup settings.

Support
If you choose to email support, the recipient receives your email address and whatever you include. No learning history is attached automatically. Please do not include information you do not want to share. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the same address.

Subscriptions
Apple handles membership payment and provides product, transaction and entitlement information to the app. An optional trial-ending reminder is scheduled locally after a verified eligible trial purchase.

Onboarding details
The app saves the learning choices listed above, notification permission choice and onboarding completion time locally. Older versions may also have saved an age band, regional context, heritage-related choice or optional free-text reason. These legacy answers are not uploaded by the current app and can be erased in Privacy & data.

Security and requests
We use platform storage and transport protections, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Contact the privacy email above to ask about information you have sent us, request correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern. Local-only learning records must be exported or deleted on your device; we cannot retrieve them remotely. Support correspondence is kept while needed to handle your request and applicable legal obligations. Please avoid including sensitive information that is not needed to resolve your request.
